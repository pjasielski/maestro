## Description

Every update to `_summary.md` currently costs a full read plus a full rewrite. Appending is cheaper and is the right default during work. Two problems block naive append-only, both solved by splitting the file into zones:

- `_summary.md` is capped at 600 words; blind appending violates the cap within a session.
- Structured sections (decisions table, tasks-touched table) break under append — rows land at the bottom instead of in their table.

Same shape applies to the decision log: append at the point of decision, reconcile at sync. This additionally gives reversals a representation they lack today — currently two contradictory decision rows sit side by side with no indication which won.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `_summary.md` has two zones: **curated** (top — structured sections, rewritten at sync) and **log** (bottom — flat chronological, append-only during work)
- [ ] During a session the agent appends to the log zone without reading the whole file
- [ ] `/sync` reads the file once, folds log entries into the curated sections, and truncates the log zone
- [ ] The 600-word cap applies to the curated zone; the log zone is exempt between syncs
- [ ] Decisions are appended with date, session, and an optional `supersedes: {id}` field
- [ ] `/sync` reconciles the decision log: **latest wins**, superseded entries marked, and **contradictions flagged** — two active decisions that disagree produce `CONSISTENCY:` and a question rather than a silent resolution
- [ ] When a reversal invalidates something downstream, `/sync` says so: *"D-021 reverses D-014, which R-007 depends on — R-007 needs review"*
- [ ] `.maestro/templates/summary.md` updated with the two-zone structure

## Files

## Notes

The contradiction flagging is the half that earns the mechanism its cost. Silent latest-wins would let a reversal quietly invalidate a requirement nobody re-checked — which is the failure this is meant to prevent, not a cheaper way to arrive at it.

This is a miniature of the ADR supersession model (M05.06). Build the vocabulary here — `supersedes`, superseded, contradiction — so M05.06 extends it rather than inventing a parallel one.
