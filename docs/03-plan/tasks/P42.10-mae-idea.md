## Description

Capturing an idea mid-task must cost nothing. `/mae-idea` parks it in `docs/01-explore/IDEAS.md` and offers exploration; it never asks. Separates *scope* (a change to what was agreed; needs a decision) from *idea* (maybe later; no decision). Source: maestro-hq `22-new-scope/15` §7.
